The first scan can take about a minute on a broad preset. It runs from the dashboard button after setup.

What happens next

  1. Scan pulls public board postings, deduplicates them, and applies your title gate.
  2. Score ranks matches with reasons, gaps, and hard-gate survival.
  3. Tailor selects verified resume facts and renders a one-page PDF.
  4. Fill opens your browser. Review every field and click Submit yourself.

No account, no telemetry, no captcha solving, and no Indeed/LinkedIn automation.

You can rerun this flow any time from the Setup link; settings remain editable in Settings.
